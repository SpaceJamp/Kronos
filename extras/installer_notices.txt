Changes from the original
-------------------------

This is a modified version of DLSS Swapper 1.2.6.1. The modifications include
renaming the application, a 64-bit-only build, an installer rewritten in Inno
Setup, and various bug fixes and additions listed under "What changed in Kronos"
in README.md. The original copyright notices are preserved in LICENSE.
